# Phase 3 Panel

This panel reduces the earlier broad CpG hit lists into a smaller mixed panel with:

- `12` general BRCA tumor markers
- `8` Basal-skewed markers
- `20` total CpGs

## Classifier Snapshot

- tumor vs normal ROC AUC mean: `0.996`
- tumor vs normal ROC AUC std: `0.003`
- Basal vs non-Basal ROC AUC mean: `0.992`
- Basal vs non-Basal ROC AUC std: `0.011`
