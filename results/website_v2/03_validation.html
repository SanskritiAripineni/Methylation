Two independent checks that the tumor–normal signal is a genuine association, not a processing artifact.

A near-perfect separation invites a fair question: is it real, or an artifact of one dataset? Two independent checks address this.

Internal — leakage audit and nested cross-validation
Markers were re-selected inside every cross-validation fold so no information leaked between training and test data. The leakage-free separation held (area under the ROC curve ≈ 0.996), indicating the signal is genuine rather than memorised.
External — independent cohort (GSE66695)
The same marker sites were examined in a separate patient group from a different laboratory. They behaved consistently — sites higher in TCGA tumors were higher here (median +0.28), and lower sites were lower (median −0.21). The association replicates across cohorts.
