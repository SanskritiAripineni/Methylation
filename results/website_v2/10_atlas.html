Per-site methylation across longevity-associated genes, as a browsable atlas.

In plain terms

Finally we zoom back to individual spots, but focus on longevity-associated genes, and build a large reference table — 43,609 spots — recording each one's methylation state in normal tissue, browsable as a spreadsheet. FGF2 is the worked example: a specific gene whose on-switch is unmethylated (active) in healthy tissue and gains methylation in tumour.

For 1,953 longevity-associated genes across 40 categories, an atlas records 43,609 individual CpG sites — each with its coordinate and its methylation state in normal tissue. The full, filterable atlas is provided as a spreadsheet (one sheet per focus category); a sample follows.

A single gene in detail — FGF2

FGF2 (Fibroblast Growth Factor 2) is a growth-signalling gene. In normal tissue its promoter is unmethylated (associated with an active gene), consistently across both cohorts; in tumor it gains methylation at those positions — a site-level illustration of the direction-of-effect logic.
