The reference methylation state of longevity-associated genes in normal tissue.

In plain terms

To say what a tumour changed, you first need a 'normal reading' to measure against. Here we record the typical methylation state of longevity-related genes in normal tissue, pooling the study's normals with an independent set. It is simply establishing the baseline before measuring the shift — like noting a room's temperature before turning on a heater.

To read tumor changes against a reference, the methylation state of longevity-associated genes was summarised in normal tissue, combining TCGA normal samples with the independent GSE66695 normals. This baseline is descriptive — a benchmark, not a batch-corrected model — and normal here means tumor-adjacent tissue rather than disease-free donor tissue.
