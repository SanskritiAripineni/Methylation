An index of the analyses behind this study. Each entry opens a short, plain-language summary followed by its figures and tables. Findings are reported as statistical associations from array data, not clinical claims.

01Study cohorts and array platformThe samples and the measurement platform that every downstream result rests on.→02Differential methylationWhere tumor and normal tissue differ in methylation, across roughly 485,000 sites.→03ValidationTwo independent checks that the tumor–normal signal is a genuine association, not a processing artifact.→04Site annotationPlacing each site in its exact genomic position and regulatory context.→05Direction of effectWhich methylation changes are consistent with a gene being turned down or up.→06Biomarker panel and sitesA prioritised set of marker sites, each mapped to a gene-level position.→07Pathway enrichmentAn exploratory gene-set analysis — reported, then re-tested with a stricter method.→08Healthy-tissue baselineThe reference methylation state of longevity-associated genes in normal tissue.→09Bias-corrected enrichmentGene-set signals after correcting for a known array design bias.→10Longevity site atlasPer-site methylation across longevity-associated genes, as a browsable atlas.→

Notes on interpretation

  • Normal tissue here is tumor-adjacent, not disease-free donor tissue.
  • Measurements are from a fixed-content array; whole-pathway enrichment is bias-prone and is reported only after correction.
  • Methylation associations are predicted effects on gene activity, not direct measurements.
