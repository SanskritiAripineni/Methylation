In plain terms

From the strongest and most interpretable changes we assemble a short, balanced shortlist — ten spots that get switched off and ten that get switched on — a candidate 'fingerprint' of a breast tumour. The chart draws each marker as a line from its methylation level in healthy tissue to its level in tumour: the longer the line, the bigger the change. Every marker is pinned to a specific gene and an exact position on the genome.

From the strongest promoter-based changes, a balanced set of marker sites was assembled — ten higher-in-tumor and ten lower-in-tumor — each tied to a gene and an exact position. The chart shows the strongest well-supported sites (at least 30 tumor and 30 normal samples).
